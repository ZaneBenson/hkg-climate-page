hkg-climate kit (read-only) - collects what the hkg-climate-page research needs

1. Copy everything in this zip to the root of an exFAT/FAT32 USB drive with at
   least 400 MB free. No other NEMESIS files on the drive.
2. If you were asked to capture a climate control: operate it on the car first,
   then run the kit within about a minute (the Android log only holds the last
   minute). Plug the drive in only after that.
3. Car on, plug in the drive, tap Yes. Wait for the message "hkg-climate kit
   finished, unplug the drive" (a few minutes: it copies about 190 MB), then
   unplug the drive.
4. The kit writes an HKGCLIMATE folder on the drive. It holds Hyundai system
   files and the Android log: keep it private and never post it. Send it only
   to the person who asked for it.

Nothing on the car is changed and no vehicle commands are sent. Accounts,
paired phone data, Wi-Fi passwords, the vehicle security partition, VIN and
serial numbers are never collected.
