hkg-climate voice kit - copies the stock voice software from the head unit

READ ONLY: it changes nothing on the car and sends no vehicle commands.

1. Copy everything in this zip to the root of a USB stick (exFAT/FAT32) with at
   least 1 GB free. It must be the only stick plugged in.
2. Car on, plug the stick in and confirm. (Or, without NEMESIS.data on the
   stick, choose "Run USB NEMESIS.sh" in NEMESIS Manager.) Wait for
   "hkg-climate voice kit finished, unplug the drive" (a few minutes: the voice
   files are large), then unplug the stick.
3. Send the HKGVOICE folder. It holds Hyundai files: keep it private and only
   send it to the person who asked for it.
