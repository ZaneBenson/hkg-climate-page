hkg-climate capture - records the Android log while you use the climate controls

READ ONLY: it changes nothing on the car and sends no vehicle commands.

1. Copy NEMESIS.sh and this file to the root of a USB stick (exFAT/FAT32) with at
   least 200 MB free (a 30-minute log is about 135 MB). Do not put a NEMESIS.data
   on it (the capture runs only from NEMESIS Manager).
2. Car on (ready to drive), stick in. Open NEMESIS Manager and choose
   "Run USB NEMESIS.sh". A message says "capture 1 started".
3. Use the stock controls one at a time, a few seconds apart, and write down what
   you pressed, in order (the head unit clock time now and then helps).
4. Open NEMESIS Manager again and choose "Run USB NEMESIS.sh". A message says
   "capture 1 stopped and saved". Unplug the stick.
   If you forget, the capture stops by itself after 30 minutes; the next run then
   just saves it.
5. Send the CAPTURE folder and your notes. Running it again starts capture 2 in
   its own folder.

CAPTURE holds the Android log (it can include phone and contact names and
Bluetooth addresses): keep it private and only send it to the person who asked.
