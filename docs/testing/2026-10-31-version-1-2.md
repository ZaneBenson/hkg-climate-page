# Version 1.2.0: on-car checklist

Version 1.2.0 follows your 1.1.0 run. Preset commands are spaced out (at least 400 ms apart), and
a temperature or fan step the car doesn't take is pressed again, at most twice (as you approved
on 2026-10-31); nothing else is ever retried. Save no longer crashes, every editor dropdown
scrolls, a preset sets the driver and passenger temperatures separately, there are six presets,
the preset you tapped stays highlighted while the car still matches it, and the panel has your
new design. Presets and settings are now kept when you update.

Package: `dist/hkg-climate-usb.zip` (the package and the kit on one stick; see its
`README-USB.txt`). Capture tool: `dist/hkg-climate-capture.zip` (see
`docs/testing/2026-10-31-climate-capture.md`). Only US firmware 260507 is supported.

Have the car on (ready to drive) for the tests. Nothing is sent at boot or when you open
settings: only a tap on a preset, a seat button or the × sends. Please note anything odd, even
if it seems small.

## Steps

1. **Update.** Write down your 1.1.0 presets first. Plug in the combined stick; NEMESIS says to
   uninstall first: dismiss that message. Restart the head unit with the stick still in. NEMESIS
   updates while the head unit starts, then restarts it once more by itself. If the climate page
   is missing or still looks the old way, restart once more with the stick in. Your presets will
   be gone this one time (1.1.0's uninstall step doesn't save them; 1.2.0's does, and step 12
   checks it): add them again from your notes.

   **Check the app itself is 1.2.0 right after the update.** NEMESIS's update at boot is best
   effort, so the new panel can end up over the old 1.1.0 app. Open settings (tap the gear) and
   add a preset: the editor must have "Driver temperature" and "Passenger temperature"
   dropdowns, and the settings screen must sit in a column at the left (Android's app info
   shows 1.2.0, if you can reach it). If it doesn't: uninstall HKG Climate Page in NEMESIS,
   restart, install again from the stick, and check once more before going on.
2. **The page**, with the climate on and both seats off: the top line shows only the outside
   temperature and a square gear button. Each seat button has a seat icon at its outer side
   (wavy arrows in heat mode with an orange border, a fan in cool mode with a blue border), the
   three bars, no seat names and no state text, and a round swap button near the top inner
   corner. Only saved presets are drawn, with no empty slots.
3. **Swap and ×.** Driver seat off: tap swap. The border and icon flip (heat to cool or back),
   and nothing changes in the car. Tap the seat: it comes on in the new mode, and the round
   button becomes ×. Tap ×: the seat turns off in one tap, and its mode goes back to the usual
   choice for the outside temperature. Do the same on the passenger seat.
4. **Settings beside the panel.** Tap the gear. With the right-hand panel still showing, the
   whole settings screen is to its left and nothing is hidden under it. Delete any old presets
   there are (there may be none; Delete asks first and the question appears on the left).
5. **Editor.** Tap to add a preset. Open each dropdown: its list opens on the left, no wider
   than the settings column, and scrolls to every value (temperatures 62 to 82). Climate Auto
   greys fan, A/C and airflow; Off greys every climate item; Sync "on" greys the passenger
   temperature. Make "Cold" (below) and tap Save: the editor closes and nothing crashes. If it
   says "Saved" but stays open, press Back and write it down.
6. **Six presets.** Add the rest; at six, Add greys out and the page shows three rows of two.
   1. "Cold": both seats heat high, steering wheel on, climate Auto, driver 72 °F,
      passenger 70 °F, Sync off.
   2. "Hot": both seats cool high, climate Manual, A/C on, fan 6, airflow face, driver 66 °F,
      Sync on.
   3. "Off": climate Off, both seats off, steering wheel off.
   4. "Eco on": Eco on, nothing else.
   5. "Eco off": Eco off, nothing else.
   6. "Seat": driver seat heat low, nothing else.
7. **Capture.** Plug in the capture stick (the one without a `NEMESIS.data`) and start the
   capture tool as in the capture checklist. Keep it running through step 10.
8. **Cold, then Hot.** Tap "Cold": it's highlighted at once (thick border, bold name, light
   fill). Seats heat high, the wheel warms, the climate goes to auto, and the temperatures step
   to exactly 72 °F (driver) and 70 °F (passenger): check both on the stock climate screen. Wait
   10 seconds: the highlight stays. Turn the driver temperature up one step with the stock
   controls: the highlight goes within about 2 seconds and doesn't come back. Then tap "Hot":
   A/C on, airflow face, fan 6, sync on, both temperatures exactly 66 °F, seats cool high, and
   the climate leaves auto. After each tap, write down the "Last command" line in settings.
9. **Hot with the climate off.** Turn the climate off with the stock off button, then tap "Hot".
   Write down what happens: does the climate come on, in auto or in manual, and which of A/C,
   airflow, fan and temperature reach their values? And the "Last command" line. The seats go
   to cool high either way.
10. **The rest.** Tap "Off": the climate, seats and wheel turn off. Tap "Seat", and watch for 10
    seconds: only the driver seat changes and the highlight stays the whole time. Tap "Eco on",
    then "Eco off": eco follows each time and matches Vehicle Settings. Tap "Seat" again: it
    highlights although nothing needed sending. Change something in a preset and Save: the
    highlight clears (saving without a change keeps it).
11. **Capture and kit.** Stop the capture tool (second run, capture stick in). Then plug in the
    combined stick and run the kit from Manager ("Run USB NEMESIS.sh") before you restart; a
    restart wipes the Android log. Send the `CAPTURE` and `HKGCLIMATE` folders with your notes:
    for each tap, what moved, what didn't, how long it took, and whether the highlight came on
    and went when it should.
12. **Update again (settings kept).** Copy the `HKGCLIMATE` folder off the combined stick. On a
    computer, rename `NEMESIS.data.installed` on it back to `NEMESIS.data`. Update as in step 1.
    Afterwards your six presets, the switch temperature and the page on/off setting are all
    unchanged, and nothing changes by itself. If anything is missing, run the kit again and send
    that `HKGCLIMATE` folder too (it holds the install log).

## If something goes wrong

- Save crashes the app, or the page goes blank after Save: write down what you'd entered, run
  the kit at once and send the folder.
- Swap changes a seat in the car, or × does anything but turn that seat off: stop, run the kit
  and send it.
- The "Last command" line names an item that didn't follow (`no change`, `send refused`,
  `send failed`, or skipped as unknown): stop tapping, run the kit and send it with the capture.
  A temperature or fan step is pressed at most three times in all, then reported.
- The highlight flickers, stays after you changed something, or doesn't come on: write down
  which preset and what you changed, and send the kit.
- The climate does something you didn't ask for (turns off, leaves auto, a value jumps): stop
  tapping, run the kit and the capture, and describe it.
- The climate page is still missing after two restarts with the stick in: uninstall HKG
  Climate Page in NEMESIS, restart, and install from the stick again, then send the kit.
- The right-hand screen goes blank or keeps restarting: uninstall HKG Climate Page in NEMESIS
  (it puts the original panel files back), then restart the unit.
- A message says the install could not be fully undone: do not restart the unit. Look at the
  NEMESIS log and tell us what it says.
