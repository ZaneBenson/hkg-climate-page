# Version 2: presets, on-car checklist

Version 2 (2.0.0) adds presets of the seat settings. A preset has a name (up to 10
characters) and, for the driver seat and the passenger seat, one of: Leave as is, Off, Heat
high, mid or low, or Cool high, mid or low (the cool choices appear for a seat that can cool,
and also while the app hasn't read the seats yet, and a cool choice stored for a seat that
can't cool is shown as "(not available on this seat)"). You can have up to four. Tapping a
preset slot on the page sets each chosen seat straight to its target, driver first. A seat
that is already there is skipped, and nothing is retried.
Package: `dist/hkg-climate-usb.zip` (recommended: the package and the kit on one stick; see
its `README-USB.txt`). Only US firmware 260507 is supported.

This installs over the build that's on the car now. Have the car on (ready to drive) for the
seat tests. Please note anything odd, even if it seems small.

## Steps

1. Install from the combined stick. If the install says there isn't enough space on /system,
   restart the head unit and plug the stick in again (the update has to finish removing the
   old build first).
2. In the Climate Page settings, under "Presets", add three presets:
   - "Cold": driver heat high, passenger heat high.
   - "Hot": driver cool high, passenger cool high.
   - "Drv off": driver off, passenger Leave as is.
3. The page shows the three names, and the fourth slot is greyed.
4. With both seats off, tap Cold: both seats go to heat high within about a second.
5. Tap Hot: both go to cool high. Tap Drv off: only the driver turns off.
6. Tap Cold twice: the second tap changes nothing, and nothing is sent for seats already
   there.
7. Right after the preset taps, and before you edit anything or restart, run the kit from
   Manager ("Run USB NEMESIS.sh" on the combined stick). The kit keeps only about the last
   minute of the Android log, and a restart wipes it. Send the `HKGCLIMATE` folder.
8. In settings, edit a preset, move one up or down, and delete one (it asks first): the page
   follows each change.
9. Restart: the presets are still there, and nothing changes by itself. Run the kit again
   after the restart only if something was odd, and send that folder too.

## If something goes wrong

- The settings screen's "Seat command" line says `no change after 3 s` and the car isn't on
  (ready to drive): this is expected. Turn the car on and tap again.
- With the car on, a preset tap leaves a seat unchanged, or the "Seat command" line says
  `no change after 3 s`, `send refused` or `send failed`: stop tapping, run the kit right away
  and send the folder. The line names the seat command(s) that didn't follow (several are
  joined with "; ").
- The right-hand screen goes blank or keeps restarting: uninstall HKG Climate Page in NEMESIS
  (it puts the original panel files back), then restart the unit.
- A message says the install could not be fully undone: do not restart the unit. Look at the
  NEMESIS log and tell us what it says.
